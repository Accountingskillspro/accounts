Vendor Reconciliation: A Practical Checklist

Use this checklist as a starting point when comparing your vendor ledger with the vendor statement.

Step 1: Collect records

Obtain the vendor statement, purchase register, invoice copies, debit notes, credit notes and payment details for the same period.

Step 2: Match transactions

Compare invoice numbers, dates, taxable values, tax amounts, credit notes and payments. Check for duplicate or missing entries.

Step 3: Identify differences

  • Invoice in your books but not on vendor statement
  • Invoice on statement but not recorded in your books
  • Payment made but not allocated by vendor
  • Credit note or debit note not matched
  • Amount, tax or date mismatch

Step 4: Follow up

Record each difference, assign an owner and request supporting documents or clarification from the vendor.

Step 5: Resolve and document

Post approved corrections, match cleared items and retain evidence of the reconciliation and review.

Control tip: Never post a correction only to make balances match. Confirm the underlying transaction and obtain required approval first.
