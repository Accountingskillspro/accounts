GST Input Tax Credit: A Beginner’s Guide

Input Tax Credit (ITC) generally means eligible GST paid on business purchases that may be set off against output GST, subject to the conditions and restrictions in the applicable law.

Practical checks

  1. Confirm the purchase is for eligible business use.
  2. Check supplier invoice details and required tax information.
  3. Reconcile purchase records with relevant GST records, including GSTR-2B where applicable.
  4. Verify receipt of goods or services and other statutory conditions.
  5. Review blocked credits, reversals and time limits that may apply.
ITC eligibility depends on the facts and current law. This article is for learning only. Confirm current requirements using official GST guidance or a qualified tax professional.

Why reconciliation matters

Matching the purchase register with invoices and GST data can reveal missing documents, tax-value differences, duplicates and supplier follow-up items.
