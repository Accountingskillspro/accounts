3-Way Matching: Purchase Order, GRN and Supplier Invoice

Three-way matching is an accounts payable control used to compare a purchase order (PO), goods receipt note (GRN), and supplier invoice before a payment is approved. It helps identify quantity differences, rate variances, duplicate keys, and missing documents.

The three documents

  • Purchase Order (PO): Shows what the business ordered, including item, quantity and agreed rate.
  • Goods Receipt Note (GRN): Records the quantity actually received.
  • Supplier Invoice: Shows the quantity and price the supplier is billing.

How the matching process works

  1. Match the PO number, PO line and item code across all documents.
  2. Compare ordered quantity with received quantity.
  3. Compare invoiced quantity with both the PO quantity and received quantity.
  4. Compare the invoice rate with the PO rate and review the amount variance.
  5. Investigate exceptions and obtain approval under your organisation’s policy before payment.

Example

If a PO is for 100 units at ₹50 each, the expected PO value is ₹5,000. If the GRN records 95 units but the supplier invoices 100 units, the invoice quantity does not agree with the received quantity. The exception should be investigated before payment approval; the correct treatment depends on the contract and company policy.

Common exceptions to investigate

  • Received quantity is lower or higher than the PO.
  • Invoice quantity differs from the GRN or PO.
  • Invoice rate differs from the agreed PO rate.
  • PO line, item code, or supporting document is missing or duplicated.
