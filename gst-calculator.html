GST Calculator

Estimate GST and invoice total from a taxable value. Choose intra-state supply to split the GST equally into CGST and SGST, or inter-state supply to calculate IGST.

Estimate only. Verify the applicable GST rate, place of supply, exemptions and rounding rules before issuing an invoice.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter the taxable value before GST.
  2. Select the applicable GST rate for your goods or services.
  3. Choose intra-state or inter-state based on the place-of-supply rules for your transaction.
  4. Review the tax split and total. This tool does not decide whether a supply is taxable or which rate applies.
Important: This is an educational estimate, not tax advice or an invoice generator. GST treatment can depend on the transaction and current law. Check official guidance and your tax professional where needed.
