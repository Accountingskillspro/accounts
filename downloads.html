Free Accounting Excel Templates

Download these simple, Excel-compatible CSV templates to practise everyday accounting tasks. Open a file in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets, enter your own data, then use Save As to keep a working copy as an Excel workbook (.xlsx) if required.

Before you begin: These are starter working sheets, not audited financial models. Review formulas, adapt them to your organisation's process, and protect confidential business data.

1. Vendor Reconciliation Template

Compare the vendor ledger with the supplier statement, highlight amount differences, record follow-up owners and track unresolved items.

Download Vendor Reconciliation CSV

2. Bank Reconciliation Template

Match cash-book transactions with the bank statement and list unmatched items for investigation.

Download Bank Reconciliation CSV

3. Monthly Cash Flow Tracker

Record receipts and payments, calculate net cash movement and track the period's cash position.

Download Cash Flow Tracker CSV

4. 3-Way Matching Excel Template

Compare Purchase Order (PO), Goods Receipt Note (GRN), and supplier invoice data. The workbook includes PO-line matching, received and invoiced quantity checks, rate and amount variances, MATCH/MISMATCH/REVIEW status, and a PO-wise summary.

Download 3-Way Matching Excel Template (.xlsx)

Includes illustrative sample data. Replace it with your own data and review exceptions against your company’s approval policy before processing payments.

How to use the templates

  1. Download the CSV file and open it with Excel or Google Sheets.
  2. Save a separate working copy before entering your real data.
  3. Check the formulas and number formats for your spreadsheet locale and workflow.
  4. Record the period, reviewer and follow-up notes where relevant.
  5. Do not upload or share confidential vendor, bank or employee information publicly.
